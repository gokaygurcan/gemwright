AI residents are lived by the v2 mind alone: v1 and its runners are removed. The `decisions` and `dungeons` commands and the `--speech`, `--decisions`, `--miner-assets`, `--fighter-assets` and `--assets` options are gone; `session --live --data <dir>` lets the mind live a resident, and `population` takes `--data`. `online.json` no longer reads `mind`, and `tuning.json` is no longer shipped. The `ai.speech`, `ai.decisions`, `ai.chaos` and `ai.wars` settings are accepted but unused for now.
