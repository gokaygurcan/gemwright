AI residents no longer stand still for good when securing their goods cannot lower a small risk: once recovery has been tried in the same situation, a healthy, unthreatened resident goes back to work, and recovery away from home walks back to its town's bank first. Ghosts look for healers only where a spawner keeps one, not at wildlife spawners a wandering healer roams from, and the AI world files no longer need a town guide.
