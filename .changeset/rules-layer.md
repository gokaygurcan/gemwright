@gemwright/common carries the ServUO rules (`game/rules.json`, generated from the pinned ServUO's scripts): item graphics and what vendors sell and buy, races and their looks, skills, maps, harvest tiles and ores, doors, spell scrolls and mana, crafting tools and stations. AI residents read every item, tool, spell, door and station from it instead of hand-written lists, which also gives gargoyles the hair styles of their own sex and the right scrolls for Arch Protection, Magic Reflection and Reveal.
