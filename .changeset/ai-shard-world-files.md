AI residents learn where vendors stand and creatures spawn, in towns, on Haven Island and overland, from the shard's own world files (Spawns/*.xml and Regions.xml), which `gemwright deploy --ai` keeps on the AI volume and replaces only when they change; `--world <path>` and `--world-pull` teach them from a world save instead, with whatever was placed or changed in game; a fighter skips any ground with a creature that attacks on sight it is not ready for.
